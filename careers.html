Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own equipment?

Yes, therapists are expected to carry their own portable massage table, oils, and towels to client locations. If you don't have equipment yet, let us know — we can discuss options during the onboarding process.

How many bookings will I get per week?

This varies based on your location, availability, and demand in your area. Full-time therapists in high-demand areas like Koramangala, Whitefield, and Indiranagar can expect regular bookings.

Can I work part-time alongside another job?

Absolutely. Many of our therapists take weekend or evening bookings alongside other work. We're flexible and understand that therapists have other commitments.

I'm a fresher with certification but no experience. Can I apply?

Yes, we encourage freshers with genuine certification and strong training to apply. You may start with simpler bookings while building your experience over time.

Which areas in Bangalore do you operate in?

We operate across all major areas including Koramangala, HSR Layout, Indiranagar, Whitefield, Marathahalli, BTM Layout, JP Nagar, Jayanagar, Electronic City, Sarjapur Road, Hebbal, Malleswaram and more.

How and when do I get paid?

Payment details are discussed during onboarding. We process payments regularly with no unnecessary delays. WhatsApp us for specifics about the current payment structure.
