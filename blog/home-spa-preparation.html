Preparing Your Space for Home Massage Service

Room Selection

Choose a quiet, comfortable room in your Bangalore home:

  • Bedroom, guest room, or study works well
  • Minimum 8x8 feet space for massage table setup
  • Away from high-traffic areas for privacy
  • Good ventilation but not directly under AC/fan
  • Dim lighting capability (natural or adjustable lights)
Pro Tip for Bangalore Homes: If you live in a studio apartment, the living area works perfectly fine for doorstep massage service. Therapists are experienced in adapting to various spaces across Bangalore.

Temperature and Ambiance

Bangalore's pleasant climate is perfect for spa at home, but set up your space properly:

Before your massage home service begins, inform your therapist about:
